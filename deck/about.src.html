About the instructor

Akylbek Maxutov

Senior Data Scientist · ISSAI, Nazarbayev University, Astana

I build and evaluate language models, mostly for Kazakh and other lower-resourced languages — and I have spent enough time inside evaluation results to distrust any single number that arrives without the apparatus that produced it.

My work runs across three connected threads: evaluation methodology — how model rankings are actually decided, and how much of a ranking is a property of the evaluator rather than the models; Kazakh-centric language models — small models, retrieval, and the cost-aware choices that make them practical; and multimodal understanding — extending vision–language models to a language with little of the data those models normally assume.

  • RoleSenior Data Scientist, ISSAI — the Institute of Smart Systems and Artificial Intelligence at Nazarbayev University.
  • ResearchLLM evaluation and LLM-as-a-judge reliability · Kazakh-centric small language models · cross-lingual retrieval · vision–language and audio–vision–language models.
  • Teaching hereTwo sessions on harness engineering and the correct use of a judge, built on a working evaluation harness written for this workshop.
